## About

I'm a third-year Information Technology student at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Industry and Trade (HUIT)**, following two connected paths: **Data & AI Engineering** and **Software Engineering**. On the machine learning side, I care about the whole lifecycle rather than the model alone: how data is collected, labeled, and cleaned, how models are trained and evaluated, and how the results are served reliably and kept maintainable in a real system.
